Swing a 3D sword
through the graveyard.

The text, buttons and text boxes on this page are plain website content — they can't be cut. Everything growing, standing and crawling at the bottom of the screen can: long grass, pumpkins, stones, tombstones… and whatever is buried underneath them.

What is this?

3D

A real sword

A 3D arming sword and a 3D long sword follow your cursor. Move fast to cut, click to slash, and watch the blade trail.

Cuttable

Almost everything

Pumpkins split into halves, quarters and seeds. Rocks break in two. Long grass falls in clumps. The grass base itself stays put.

Undead

Don't break the tombstone

Smash a tombstone and a hole opens in the ground — a zombie claws its way out and goes straight for your sword.

Physics

Throw rocks

Grab rocks (right-drag or grab mode) and fling them. They hit pumpkins, tombstones and zombies for low damage.

Controls

MouseMove the sword. Swing quickly across something to cut it.
ClickSlash (alternates left-to-right, right-to-left and a diagonal chop).
Right-drag / Shift+dragGrab a rock, pumpkin or tombstone and throw it.
WheelChange how deep the blade reaches (scroll back to reach the trees and the dragon in the back row).
GToggle grab mode (left mouse grabs instead of cuts — handy on touch screens).
1 / 2Arming sword / long sword.
RReset the whole graveyard.
DOpen or close the dev profile.
SpaceSlow motion while held.

The world

Long grass

Grows in the corners and along the edges. It never hides a zombie, and it slowly grows back.

Pumpkins

Slice them any way you like — horizontal cuts and vertical cuts split differently.

Rocks & stones

Corner stones are part of the scenery until you cut or grab them. Thrown rocks do low damage.

Zombies

They limp, lunge, bite your sword and can lose arms, legs and heads. Hit the head for big damage.

Notes (a text box that cannot be cut)

Put it on GitHub

This is a single file. Save it as index.html in a repository, enable Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch, and it is live. The only outside file it loads is three.js from a CDN.
